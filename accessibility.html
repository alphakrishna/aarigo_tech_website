In short: we aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA, we build it in from the start, and if anything gets in your way we want to hear about it.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, the standard most accessibility laws point to.

What we've built in

  • Text sizes follow your browser setting, so you can make the text larger.
  • Every page works with a keyboard, with a "Skip to main content" link and a clear outline on whatever you've selected.
  • Text and background colours are checked against the level AA contrast ratios.
  • Images that carry meaning have a text description. Decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Every form field has a visible label.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, our page animations stay still.
  • There are no tracking scripts or third party overlays on the site.

Known limits

  • The demo app on our Services and Mobile apps pages plays on a loop. To keep it still, turn on reduce motion in your device settings.
  • We are still testing the site with screen readers. If something doesn't read well, please tell us.

Tell us what isn't working

If anything on this site is hard to use, tell us what happened and on which page. Email hello@aaurigotech.com or use the form on our Contact page. We'll reply within one business day and, where we can, fix it.

How we check

We check the site with automated tests and by hand, using a keyboard and zoomed in text. This statement was last reviewed on [PLACEHOLDER date].
