Work · Case study · Website

From a slow site to a story told in full.

The story of an expert whose website hid their work, and how we brought all of it into view.

Chapter 01

The start

An expert with years of real work had a website that took over 7 seconds to load. Most visitors left before they ever saw that work.

A website is often the first meeting. It should show your best work.

Chapter 02

The wall

Rebuilding a personal brand raises hard questions:

  • How do you fit years of work on one site?
  • Who writes it, when you are busy doing the work?
  • How do you make it fast without losing the detail?
  • How do you show the person, not just the CV?

This is where good work gets lost.

Chapter 03

Why us

They didn't need a new template. They needed one team to research their work, write their story, design it and build it fast, without taking their time.

That's where we came in.

Chapter 04

Making it real

  1. We studied their work

    Everything they had published, their posts online and their old site.

  2. We wrote every page

    So the site tells their full story in their own voice.

  3. We designed the story

    A moving headline, a running list of highlights, and each part of their work appearing gently as you scroll.

  4. We made it fast

    Fast on every screen, with one step to get in touch.

Chapter 05

Still with us.
