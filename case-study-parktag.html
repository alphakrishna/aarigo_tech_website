From a great idea to its first 1,000+ users.

The story of one founder, one everyday problem, and how we turned an idea into a product people use.

Chapter 01

The idea

A founder came to us with an idea for a problem people meet every day. No brand, no product and no website yet. Just the idea.

Every product people love started as one small idea.

Chapter 02

The wall

But an idea is not a product. Between the two stood questions every founder knows:

  • What should it be called, and how should it look?
  • How do you turn it into something people can actually use?
  • Who builds it, and how fast?
  • How much do you spend before you even know people want it?

This is where most good ideas stop.

Chapter 03

Why us

The founder didn't need five different suppliers. They needed one team that could shape the brand, design the product, build it and launch it, and keep it realistic: the right first version, not the most expensive one.

That's where we came in.

Chapter 04

Making it real

  1. We sat down together

    We worked through every part of the idea with the founder, before anything was built.

  2. We gave it a name and a face

    We shaped the brand and how it stands out.

  3. We built the smallest version that could prove it

    From scratch, so the budget went into what real users needed, not into guesses.

  4. We made it fast and simple

    A web app that opens in under half a second, with nothing to install, ready to sell and to grow.
