Mobile app questions.

Don't see yours? Book a call and ask us directly.

How long does a mobile app take?

It depends on what it needs to do. Once the plan is ready, you get a date for every milestone.

Do I need my own app store accounts?

Yes, so the app is always yours. Each store charges its own fee, which you pay directly. We walk you through the setup.

What if a store asks for changes?

We prepare everything to their rules and handle anything they come back with, so you don't have to.

Can it do more later?

Yes. Premium adds areas for each type of user, maps or AI features, subscriptions and usage dashboards, and we can add more in later milestones.

Who pays for the servers and services it uses?

You do, directly to each provider. Every account is in your name, so nothing is ever locked to us.

Who owns the code?

You do. Once it's paid for, the code, the design and every account are yours.
