How much does a website cost in Australia?

Real 2026 price levels in Australian dollars, what changes the price, the running costs people forget, and what we charge.

The short answer

Most Australian businesses pay between A$1,500 and A$10,000 for a business website, depending on how many pages it has and how much is designed just for them. Online stores and sites with custom features usually start around A$6,000 and can pass A$25,000. A custom website of up to 6 pages with us starts from A$3,999.

Website prices in Australia, by level

A website can be anything from a template you fill in yourself to a custom build with bookings and payments. These are the five levels you will see when you ask around.

What changes the price

  • How many pages. Every page is designed, written for and tested. Six strong pages usually do more for you than twenty thin ones.
  • Custom design or template. A custom design shows people the real quality of your business. A template looks like everyone else using it.
  • Who writes the words and supplies the photos. If you have them ready, the build is faster and costs less.
  • Features. Bookings, payments, member areas and connections to other tools add work. If people need to sign in to get something done, you may need a web app instead. See website or web app: which does your business need?
  • Accessibility and speed. A site everyone can use, that opens fast, takes care to build, and it pays you back in enquiries.
  • Who changes it after launch, and what each change costs.

How to avoid paying twice

  • Ask who owns it. The code, the design and every account should be yours once it is paid for. With us, they are.
  • Ask what happens after launch, and what each change costs.
  • See it on your phone before you sign off. Most of your visitors will.
  • Check the domain is in your name, not the builder's.
  • Compare like with like. Ask every builder for the same list: pages, who writes the words, features, and the cost of the first year after launch.

Questions

Why do website prices vary so much in Australia?

Because a website can be anything from a template you fill in yourself to a custom build with bookings and payments. Compare the same list of pages and features, not just the total.

Is a cheap website a false economy?

It can be, if it looks like many other sites, loads slowly or can't be found in search. The price that matters is what the site brings in, not what it costs once.

How long does a website take?

With us, a website of up to 6 pages is built in one week, after one week of prep together.

Do I have to pay every month?

Only for what you choose. Your domain, hosting and email go straight to those providers, and our care plan is optional.

Can I add an online store later?

Yes. We plan the site so it can grow, and a new feature gets its own price first.
