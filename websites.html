Ready before we build.

Before we start, we send you one simple checklist: every photo, text and login we need, with the size and quality each photo should have. If something is missing, we tell you where to get it.

Your build week is booked once everything is in. If something is still on its way, your project waits safely with us and starts at the next free slot.

Two weeks of care, included.

Your package includes two weeks of our care plan after launch. In that time we make the small changes for you: swapping a photo, updating some text, adding or removing something on a page. Bigger changes, like a new look or a new section, become a small project of their own, with a clear price first.

Website questions.

Don't see yours? Book a call and ask us directly.

What if I don't have photos or text yet?

Send us the facts about your business and we shape the words with you. For photos, our checklist tells you exactly what to take and the quality each one needs.

Can I have more than 6 pages?

Yes. Premium adds up to 15 custom pages, with blog or product pages. Or tell us what you need and we quote it on top.

Can I edit the website myself?

We keep your website free of an editing panel, so nothing can slow it down or break it. Every change is made by us instead.

What does it cost to run?

Can you rebuild my current website?

Yes. We tell you honestly whether to improve it or start fresh.

Who owns the website?

You do. Once it's paid for, the code, the design and every account are yours.
