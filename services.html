Focus. Build. Grow.

The same three phases for every website, web app and mobile app. We walk every step with you, and ask only for the little that only you can give.

Websites take one week of prep, then one week to build, with up to 6 custom pages. Web apps and mobile apps are built in milestones, with the timeline set in your plan. The build starts once your content is in and the plan is approved.

  1. Onboard

    01 · Focus

    We start by getting to know you and your business over a relaxed 30 minute call. Once the paperwork is done, we set you up with your own project document, and the fun begins.

    All we need: one call, and a yes to the plan.

  2. Plan and content

    02 · Focus

    Together we plan every page or screen, every feature, the words and the photos. You share what only you know, and we shape the rest so it sounds like you.

    All we need: your logo, photos and the facts about your business, in one go.

  3. Prep call

    03 · Focus

    Before we build, we sit down with you and walk through the whole plan, so we start ready. Nothing is built until you're happy with it.

    All we need: one call, and your approval.

  4. Build

    04 · Build

    This is where your idea comes to life. Watch it come together, milestone by milestone, on a live preview link or on your own phone.

    VIP support for the entire build period

    All we need: your changes in one list, across three review rounds.

  5. Launch

    05 · Build

    We check everything on every screen, then we launch it together. It is a big day, and we celebrate it with you.

    All we need: your final yes.

  6. Handover

    06 · Grow

    We don't hand over the keys and walk away. You get training, a written guide and full ownership, and our care plan is there whenever you need us.

    Nothing needed from you. You get back to your business.

Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Book a call and ask us directly.

How can you deliver this fast without cutting quality?

We scope tightly around one core outcome, work in focused sprints and reuse proven building blocks. We cut scope from version one, never quality.

How does launching early save me money?

Real users show you what matters. Instead of paying to build every feature up front, you invest in what the data proves people use and pay for.

How much does it cost?

Websites start from A$4,999, web apps from A$24,999 and mobile apps from A$34,999. A brand can be added to any of them for A$2,999. Need more pages, a bigger scope or something different, like a custom platform or a system that connects to yours? We quote it on top, never below, and no work starts until you've agreed the price.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, on every service. Pay in one go, or split the price into two or three payments spread across the build, whichever suits your cash flow best.

How quickly can we talk?

In as little as 30 minutes. Book a call and pick any open slot during your business hours, shown in your own time zone.

How involved do I need to be?

A kickoff, a few short calls to lock the scope, and a review call at each milestone. You can watch progress on your preview link any time. We handle the rest.

Do you work with clients in other countries?

Yes. Aaurigo Tech is a global company, and we work across time zones with clear updates.

What happens after launch?

We don't walk away. We train your team, hand over a written guide and full ownership, and look at the real data with you. If you want us to keep looking after it, our care plan makes every change for you.

Do you work with existing websites or apps?

Yes. We can improve, rebuild or extend what you already have. We'll tell you honestly whether it's faster to improve or to start fresh.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes, with our care plan: every change made, tested and kept fast by the people who built it. It starts at A$449 a month, paid in advance. More complex projects need more care, so the rate goes up with them, and we agree it with you first.

Is what you build accessible?

Yes, by default: readable contrast, full keyboard use, and it works with screen readers and reduced motion settings. It's the right thing to do, it reaches more customers, and it lowers legal risk.

Who owns the code?

You do. Once the project is paid for, the code, designs and accounts are yours.
