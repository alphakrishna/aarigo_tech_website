Who helps us

We use a small number of trusted providers. They handle your data only on our instructions:

  • Zoho: our business email, booking calls and the newsletter
  • Google: booking calls and holding video calls
  • Cloudflare: hosting, analytics without cookies, and spam protection
  • Web3Forms: delivers the Contact page form to our inbox

Some of these providers store data outside your country, including outside the EU and the UK. Where they do, they use approved safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and bookings that don't become a project: 12 months.
  • Client records: for the length of the project, then as long as tax and legal rules require ([PLACEHOLDER years]).
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.
  • Business contact details used to offer our services: 6 months if unused, otherwise 12 months after we last contacted you.
  • If you ask us not to contact you, we keep only your email address on a "do not contact" list, so we never email you again.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, to stop contacting you, or to object to how we use it. Email privacy@aaurigotech.com and we'll reply within one month. Every email we send includes a way to opt out.

If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Security

We protect your data with strong passwords, two step sign in and access limited to the people who need it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
